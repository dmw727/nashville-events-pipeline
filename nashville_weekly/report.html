How to read this report

Dates use America/Chicago. Unknown ages, prices and registration requirements stay unknown. “Free” requires an explicit free or zero-price field; free parking or free admission for younger children does not qualify. Family wording is a useful signal, not a guarantee of eligibility.

Drive estimates use a public downtown reference point, source coordinates where available, otherwise a city center. The estimate is straight-line miles × 1.3 ÷ 40 mph + 10 minutes. It does not use road routing or traffic. City-center estimates can substantially understate travel within Nashville. A 75-minute collection boundary allows a buffer around the requested hour; check an actual route before committing.

Multi-day listings may have closures or separate sessions. Registration, prices, cancellations and availability can change after collection. Open the source before making plans. No browser tracking, external images, analytics, LLM calls or child data are used.
